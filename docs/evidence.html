GEMSDOE29 leakage — formal diagnosis (Kaufman et al.)

Full write-up: docs/leakage-review.md. In one line: the feature log1p(min(distance-to-catalogue, 60)) was computed from the full label raster while training labels were the catalogue's own pixels, so the feature is exactly 0 on every positive (our X4 positive control measures separability 1.000 for this construction) — target information that cannot legitimately exist for an unmapped fault at prediction time. Fix = learn–predict separation: every catalogue-derived feature is recomputed from visible faults only, which is what H8 does (X4 verifies it).

Limitations (46 items)

  • L-39: Compute: 2 vCPU, ~3.9 GB RAM, no GPU. The template CNN pipeline cannot be trained here; HGB models only.
  • L-40: No organizer feedback loop: this session cannot submit. Every score here is HOLDOUT-DTI or USER-REPORTED; the expected leaderboard score of the shipped file is unknown.
  • L-41: The full uniqueness gate takes ~20 min on 2 CPUs (1,222 files, 605 unique on-grid). It ran after the build, so the duplicate verdict came after the last experiment budget was spent.
  • L-42: Session 2026-10-09 produced no OK-to-submit file. The single E3 build was the only build the 3-experiment budget allowed.
  • L-90: The proposed next-session H11 sources (INGENIOUS 2 m magnetics, paleo-geothermal surface-vent deposits, springs; S26) were not downloaded or licence-checked in this session; the pinned INGENIOUS mirror (S26) is the only verified holder of the data (irregularity IR-53-44). The spatially blocked holdout for H11 (posterior P(vent|paleo, spring)) is untested.
  • L-91: USGS Faulds 2013 (S91) and the GRC 2013 abstract (S92) support the plausibility of the H8 tip/relay mechanism using Great Basin field statistics (32% of fields in relays/step-overs, 22% at tip-terminations). They do not validate performance on the competition's unmapped faults.
  • L-92: Single seed (53) and two-vCPU compute. The H8-vs-ridge comparison is paired over the same five spatial folds (t interval of the paired difference, K=5), and the pooled per-fold interval is reported; a multi-seed robustness sweep was not run.
  • L-93: H10 (profile-curvature stratigraphy) was evaluated and cut (negatively) on fold 0 of the holdout. H12 (tectonic anisotropy) is proposed and untested; it needs a spatially blocked holdout before any use of a submission slot. H11 remains unrun (see L-90).
