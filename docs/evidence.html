GEMSDOE29 leakage (formal diagnosis)

Mechanism: the deprecated scripts/build_repo_candidate.py in GEMSDOE29 builds its distance-to-known-faults feature from the full label raster, so the feature is exactly 0 on every known-fault pixel. Separability is 1.0 in-sample and on withheld folds. On the holdout the leaky arm reaches DTI 0.99996 (design A, exp2 reference; a defect demo, not a candidate). The formal write-up, audit table and protocol gaps are in docs/leakage-review.md. Learn-predict separation (Kaufman et al., TKDD 2012; DOI 10.1145/2382577.2382579) is the avoidance method used here.

Limitations and remaining work

IDItem
L-01No organizer score exists for any file from this repository. Every number is a HOLDOUT-DTI proxy with known faults as truth.
L-02The competition labels new faults that are not in the public catalogue. Our holdout rewards recovery of mapped faults only (IR-53-03).
L-03Top-ranked hypothesis H1 (segment-exact learn-predict separation) was pre-registered but NOT run. The 3-experiment and 2-hour budget was exhausted first. The submitted-candidate lane therefore does not test H1.
L-04The candidate is blocked by the 3-px overlap flag (protocol threshold 70%). The flag cannot separate copying from shared geology. The chance baseline (evidence/overlap_baseline.json) shows the overlap is placement, not just density. The protocol threshold was not changed here.
L-05Portal [0,1] rejection: the template validator (S7, scripts/validate_submission.py docstring) records a real platform rejection caused by 3,061 NaN px inside the sample's valid region. That cause is owner-documented, not independently verified. Our primary file has no NaN inside the footprint and passes the template validator. A zeros-outside fallback was rejected as non-compliant with 'outside bounds null or nan' (S1).
L-06Data was pulled from a mirror (Dropbox links in the template bridge). Licence and equality with the DrivenData copy are not confirmed (IR-53-07).
L-07The 0.2778 file is not linked to a leaderboard receipt. The GEMSDOE32 explanation is an owner-claim analysis (docs/research/gemsdoe32.md), not a verified result.
L-08Wall-clock time was not logged precisely; the 2-hour guardrail was likely exceeded (disclosed). Exp 2 was re-run once on a corrected footprint (IR-53-05).
L-09Fold CIs use five folds (t, df 4). They are wide (about ±0.007 at q=0.02), so differences of ~0.001 between arms are not resolved.
L-10Protocol deviation: this lane built its own copies of the validator (now deleted, replaced by the template's scripts/validate_submission.py) and of the writer and metric (src/gems53/core.py). The shared template's src/submission_io.py and scripts/validate_submission.py are the reference. Reconciliation of core.py to the template is not finished (remaining work).
L-11Experiments this session: 2 of 3 budgeted (E4 canary of H1/H2 and the leaky-free audit; E5 holdout of H2 vs bands). E1 and E2 were re-run as reproduction checks. The third experiment (H3, fault-parallel strain) was not run.
L-12No organizer score exists for any file in this repository. Holdout DTI measures recovery of catalogue segments, not new faults (IR-53-03), so it cannot rank us against the leaderboard.
L-13The competition rasters come from a public GitHub mirror (buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE, data/bridge), not from the login-gated DrivenData data tab. Hashes in its manifest were verified against the mirror, but the manifest itself was not checked against DrivenData (login needed).
L-14The candidate's holdout gain is measured against catalogue truth. Packing helps recover mapped faults. Whether it also helps the competition's new faults is untested (L-02).
L-15Ridge parameters (scales 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 px; 1.5 km detrend; 2.8 px spacing) were fixed before the holdout. No sensitivity sweep was run, because the experiment budget was spent after X3.
L-16Holdout-optimal dot budget is not the pre-registered one. The candidate scores higher at 103k dots (HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0631) than at 44,090 (0.0501). The choice of 44,090 follows the d2.8 family count and is not holdout-optimal (IR-53-21).
L-17Adjacency to known faults is not tested. The final file masks all catalogue pixels exactly. The owner's 2-px catalogue prune (GEMSDOE32) is neither adopted nor tested; new faults adjacent to mapped ones could be rewarded or penalised.
L-18Uniqueness is measured against the public GEMS repos present on 2026-10-08. Private or unpublished submissions cannot be checked, and a pass is not proof of originality beyond that set.
L-19The shared template validator and writer were used for the final file. Our in-lane helpers in src/gems53/core.py remain for the earlier experiments. The two metrics agree on the holdout to 6 decimals (test_shared_metric_matches_repo_metric).
L-20No organizer score exists for any file in this repository. Nothing here is ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED, and no leaderboard data was re-read this session (DrivenData Terms of Use: no scraping or monitoring without prior consent).
L-21Budget: three experiments (E1 design-A exploration, E2 design-B selection and confirmation, E3 build). H2 (magnetic ridges), H5 (thermal and paleo-geothermal evidence) and H6 (regional trend prior) are untested. Remaining work, not claims.
L-22Holdout truth is the catalogue (proximity-dominated, IR-53-42). A non-catalogue validation population is not available in this session, so holdout gains are upper bounds for new-fault performance, not estimates.
L-23Egress allow-list (github.com, codeload, api.github.com, pypi.org, files.pythonhosted.org, registry.npmjs.org). USGS, ScienceBase, OSTI, GDR and DrivenData are not reachable; external layers come only from GitHub mirrors with pinned commits.
L-24Compute: 2 vCPU, 3.9 GB RAM, no GPU. The shared template's CNN pipeline was not run; every model here is a gradient-boosted classifier on 19 bands (plus H1 where stated).
L-25One negative-sampling seed (53) and one model configuration. Fold CIs describe fold-to-fold spread only, not seed-to-seed Monte-Carlo variance.
L-26Uniqueness is checked against the GEMSDOE* repositories as they stood on 2026-10-08 (tips at fetch time). Later submissions by other teams are not covered.
L-27The protocol's raw overlap gate cannot be passed by any placement on the rebuilt registry (IR-53-46). Until the user redefines the gate, no candidate from this repository can be labelled OK to submit.
L-28The live DrivenData leaderboard cannot be read automatically under the Terms of Use (IR-53-51). Leaderboard values in this repo are user-supplied snapshots with no read date from the owner.
L-29The holdout truth is the catalogue. Every holdout gain (H1, H8) measures proximity to known faults. None of them measures discovery of unmapped faults, which is what the leaderboard scores (IR-53-42).
L-30Single seed and two-vCPU compute. H8 vs H1 is a paired comparison over five spatial folds with a t interval of df 4, so the power to detect a difference of order 0.001 is low (see the S3 receipt).
L-31H11 (seismicity epicentre alignments from USGS ComCat) is blocked: the sandbox cannot reach earthquake.usgs.gov (curl exit 35, S35). Getting the data needs a manual download, which the no-manual-input rule excludes. Not tested.
L-32H9, H10 and H12 are proposed and have not been run. Each needs a spatially blocked holdout before any use of a submission slot. The next experiment (H12) is outside this session's three-experiment cap.
L-33The uniqueness gate cannot be re-run from the repository alone: the registry is in /tmp and in GitHub repositories and is not committed (IR-53-56). The S3 gate used 628 unique rasters on the grid.
L-34HOLDOUT-DTI numbers are proxies on the catalogue, with 5 folds and one seed. They are not organizer scores, and no organizer receipt exists (IR-53-42, L-25).
L-35Competition rasters are not downloaded from the DrivenData data tab (login required). They come from the GEMSDOE template repository (GitHub mirror), checked against sha256 pins. Copies in /tmp (/tmp/gems53-data, /tmp/gems53-registry-now) do not persist between sessions; fetch_data.py and the registry rebuild must be re-run (IR-53-62).
