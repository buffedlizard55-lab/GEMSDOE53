GEMSDOE29 leakage (formal diagnosis)

Mechanism: the deprecated scripts/build_repo_candidate.py in GEMSDOE29 builds its distance-to-known-faults feature from the full label raster, so that feature is exactly 0 on known-fault pixels. This is target-derived training information, not a valid accuracy result. In our separate historical design-A leaky ablation (not GEMSDOE29's organizer score), the pooled HOLDOUT-DTI was 1.0000, 95% t CI (df 4) [0.999955, 0.99996]; evaluator gems53.core.dti v1.0.0, 60,988 withheld positives in 3,199 segments. This near-perfect diagnostic is invalid for model-quality claims because the negative-pool buffer depended on withheld labels (IR-53-37). GEMSDOE29's upstream TRAIN-AUC and this reproduction are distinct evidence. See docs/leakage-review.md; learn-predict separation is the repair.

Limitations and remaining work

IDItem
L-01No ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED submission score exists for any candidate in this repository. Reported model results are HOLDOUT-DTI proxies against withheld catalogue faults; non-score quantities (counts, hashes, dimensions) are not scores.
L-02The competition labels new faults that are not in the public catalogue. Our holdout rewards recovery of mapped faults only (IR-53-03).
L-03H1 was tested in E1/E2 (design B for decisions). Stage 1 selected among 24 variants and is optimistic; stage 2 used spatially contiguous super-regions and met the pre-registered paired rule. Neither result validates performance on new, non-catalogued faults (IR-53-42).
L-04The current candidate fails the literal raw 3-px overlap rule (70% threshold): 99.599% of its final dots fall within 3 px of the GEMSDOE13 r13 lattice, while chance coverage is 99.87% (lift 0.9973). This is a protocol duplicate/STOP, not evidence of byte-identical copying or excess placement (IR-53-46).
L-05Portal [0,1] rejection: the template validator (S7, scripts/validate_submission.py docstring) records a real platform rejection caused by 3,061 NaN px inside the sample's valid region. That cause is owner-documented, not independently verified. Our primary file has no NaN inside the footprint and passes the template validator. A zeros-outside fallback was rejected as non-compliant with 'outside bounds null or nan' (S1).
