  • Population: 614 single-band registry rasters on the competition grid, re-mirrored 2026-10-08 from the 910 public tif files (629 unique; IR-53-27).
  • Literal rule (rho > 0.90 or > 70% of our dots within 3 px of one registry raster's dots): 175 files flagged. This rule is unsatisfiable for any nonzero submission (IR-53-26): registry rasters exist whose dots cover (nearly) the whole footprint — dense probability surfaces, a uniform lattice submission, dense lineament-network rasters — so every dotted raster overlaps them ~100%. The literal flags are reported unchanged for the record.
