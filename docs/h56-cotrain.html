Disagreement as the discovery signal

View A (potential-field + subsurface) vs View B (surface). The fault may be buried where A is confident and B abstains; where B is confident and A abstains, suspect roads or erosion lines. The test is the correlation of the two views’ spatial-block out-of-fold errors on labeled negatives.

Co-training, step by step (Blum & Mitchell COLT ’98, doi:10.1145/279943.279962)

View sufficiency & conditional independence are assumptions the prompt asks to TEST, not to assume. The old evidence omitted all OOF negative predictions and could not establish independence. H56 tests it on spatial blocks.

  1. Two views: View A is gravity (bands 13,11,18,5), magnetics (1,2,3,9,14), strain (4,7,8), seismicity (10,16), depth to basement (15), conductivity (17). View B is detrended elevation (12), slope (19), DEM-derived curvature/line response, plus any radiometric bands in training_features.tif — which resolves to band 6: the file’s tag says “magnetic_data / tilt angle” but measured Spearman vs GeoDAWN TC is 1.000 and vs K+Th+U is 0.9914, while |ρ| ≤0.149 vs all five magnetic bands. So band 6 is GeoDAWN radiometric total count, not a magnetic derivative, and belongs in View B (IR-52-019, bytes proof). Plus 6 external GeoDAWN bands (K,Th,U,Th/K,U/K,U/Th) and LiDAR scarp.
  2. Independence test (empirical): 50×50 blocks, ≥32 labeled negatives per block (footprint pixels 300 m clear of any catalogue trace, because a 300 m kernel would otherwise teach that the trace is background — positive-unlabelled). For each block compute per-view mean squared error (MSE = mean(p²) on negatives) and false positive rate at 0.5, then correlate View A vs View B errors across blocks. Both Pearson and Spearman are checked; if max |r| ≥0.60, abandon co-training (ABANDON_R=0.60). H56 synthetic: 4672 blocks, max |r|=0.0075 → proceed (weak proxy correlation, not proof of conditional feature independence).
  3. Pseudo-label only where one view is confident and the other abstains, with spatial isolation: abstain = ≤0.60 quantile on the unlabeled pool (withholds, not “believes opposite”); confident = ≥0.995 quantile. Keep only whole 50×50 components where every pixel of the confident component lies inside the fit region (not within 80 px buffer of a block boundary) and where the receiver is in its abstain interval (not confidently negative). Caps: 2000 px per round, min 5 px per component, at least 200 pseudo-labels per view to avoid noise. This prevents leakage that a 300 m kernel would forgive.
  4. Disagreement as discovery: strata via per-view quantiles (conf 0.98, abstain 0.60): concordant (both confident), A-only (A confident, B abstains → buried beneath cover, flag for Phase 2 reasoning), B-only (B confident, A abstains → suspect road/erosion/levee to suppress), silent. Density for emission: 0.7×A-only×(0.5+0.5×cover) + 0.25×concordant + 0.05×B-only×0.3 plus 1% base View A, where cover = log1p(depth)/ (1+slope/10). B-only is down-weighted 20×.
  5. Compare vs single-view baseline on hide-and-recover: 4 quadrant whole-component folds with 80 px buffer, same footprint, same logistic learner (L2, C=1.0, balanced), same metric kernel. H56 synthetic demo reports View B 0.039 vs cotrain 0.097 illustratively; a real test must show mean lift ≥0.005 and ≥3/4 folds positive to promote.
  6. Normalize, place, gate, not-union: rank/emit to [0,1], then metric-aware greedy_emit (triangular kernel R=3 px =300 m, k(d)=max(1-d/300,0)) maximizing expected coverage surrogate Σ ρ_g max_x k(d) under fixed budget (here 37,654). Then format gate, uniqueness gate (all priors, ≥20% novelty, selective), and not-union (vs topK and vs greedy unions).
  7. Phase-2 readiness: for every A-only 300-m neighborhood, a row with East/North, cover, probs, stratum counts, and a geological claim + alternative (e.g., “basin-fill burial vs lithologic contact”). See CSV (372 A-only groups).

Why this can catch a fault missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue

The catalogue is built from surface expression (INQUA QFaults, INGENIOUS mapping of scarps). Where a range-front splays under an alluvial fan, the DEM scarp is gone but the gravity/mag step + radiometric Th/K step survives, because the two materials still sit either side of the plane. A- confident / B-abstain isolates exactly those; a catalogue fault is concordant (both views confident) and scores in a different stratum. Surface artifacts (roads, canals, quarry faces, erosion lines) do the opposite: B-only, suppressed.

Limitations (not hidden)

  • Synthetic data: View A/B in this H56 file are simulated; a real submission needs real training_features.tif bands and derived layers. The synthetic holdout is illustrative.
  • Proxy negatives: “labeled negatives” are catalogue-zero proxies, not verified absences (positive-unlabelled).
  • Weak correlation ≠ proof: low block-error correlation is a proxy diagnostic for co-training compatibility, not a proof of conditional independence or view sufficiency.
  • No organizer score: no portal upload or organizer score is claimed for H56.
