# H55 research register — hypotheses, rank, and execution gate

Registered 2026-10-07 UTC before any H55 model fit or spatial holdout. H55-1 is an **untested execution of R2-H2**, not a claim of conceptual novelty to this repository or to geology. Exact settings and the no-slot decision are frozen in [`registry/h55_paired_shoulders_preregistration.json`](../registry/h55_paired_shoulders_preregistration.json), inheriting the spatial protocol in [`registry/r2_preregistration.json`](../registry/r2_preregistration.json).

## Status and decision

The core competition rasters were restored from the SHA-256/byte-count-pinned owner mirrors listed in `registry/data_manifest.json`. The pins confirm those mirrored bytes; they do **not** authenticate them against the organizer's login-walled portal. The fail-closed preflight receipt is `evidence/h55_paired_shoulders_protocol_gate.json`.

No H55 submission TIFF is built by this research track. H55-1's first experiment is a **fresh `view_B` refit on the same four spatial folds, training rows, learner settings and seed**. Inherited folds hold out whole original 8-connected catalogue components, assign them to four contiguous quadrants, and apply an 80-pixel training buffer; catalogue-zero is an unverified absence proxy. At the matched primary budget of 37,654 pixels, H55-1 must exceed matched `view_B` mean DTI by at least 0.005 and have positive paired lift in at least three of four folds. Failure, incomplete folds, or an integrity issue means research-only; do not spend a weekly upload slot.

**Observed registered result:** the SHA-pinned input/protocol preflight passed. H55-1 produced mean local metric DTI `0.1970317574` versus `0.1946708778` for the fresh matched View-B refit, a paired mean lift of **+0.0023608796**, with positive paired lift in **4/4** folds. The frozen gate required at least +0.005 and 3/4 positive folds, so the scientific gate **failed on the mean-lift threshold**. Do not spend a weekly slot. The fold receipts, training-row counts, paired scores, hashes, and limitations are in [`evidence/h55_paired_shoulders_holdout.json`](../evidence/h55_paired_shoulders_holdout.json); the exact input/source pins are in [`evidence/h55_paired_shoulders_run_integrity.json`](../evidence/h55_paired_shoulders_run_integrity.json).

The frozen registration uses the phrase “mean official DTI”; its result is the competition metric formula applied locally to owner-mirror catalogue labels, not an organizer-run evaluation or leaderboard score. This wording clarification does not change the registered candidate, data, folds, model, gate, or result. Owner mirror hashes passed, but organizer input-byte authentication remains false. No H55 TIFF was built and no portal action was performed.

Even a passing local proxy gate would not authenticate a public score, prove fault truth, or itself authorize a slot. Full-inventory decoded uniqueness, independent confirmation, the [0,1]/grid TIFF read-back, A-only reasoning, source/sponsor-sharing review, and organizer acceptance remain separate gates.

## Ranked hypotheses

Rank is a qualitative expected out-of-block DTI-per-cost judgment. **No numeric public-score gain is forecast.** The mechanism is a testable idea, not evidence that a fault exists or that a proposed pixel is correct.

| Rank / candidate | Layers | Physical signature / why catalogue-missing faults might be found | Difference from prior work / alternatives | Expected DTI / cost / data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — H55-1 paired DEM shoulders** (R2-H2) | Training band 12 (detrended elevation) and band 19 (slope); transform uses band 12 only. | Bilateral samples at 2, 4 and 6 pixels (200, 400, 600 m) on the local DEM gradient normal; signed second difference, balanced relief and side asymmetry. A small fan scarp or secondary splay omitted from heterogeneous maps may persist where absolute curvature is weak. | Existing View B has multi-scale pointwise curvature; no comparable fixed-offset paired-shoulder arm has been measured. This is not new-to-repo: R2-H2 registered the idea. Roads, gullies, terraces and erosion can mimic it; 100 m cells cannot resolve metre-scale scarps. | **Moderate possible lift, high uncertainty; no forecast.** Low-to-medium cost. Core data only; no new external source required. |
| **2 — H55-2 conductivity-edge termination / cross-field intersection** (R2-H3) | Bands 17 (conductivity surface), 13/18 (gravity), 2 (RTP magnetics), 15 (modelled basement depth). | Persistent conductivity-edge endpoints/crossings where a potential-field normal has a different orientation; test edge geometry rather than conductivity highs. A covered structural intersection or alteration boundary might accompany an unmapped fault. | Existing code ranks conductivity/gradients, not this registered endpoint/crossing geometry. Clay, salinity, lithology, smoothing and correlated gravity-derived layers are serious alternatives; band semantics/units require review. | **Low-to-medium possible lift, high uncertainty; no forecast.** Medium cost. Distributed deformation may include minor splays omitted at map scale. | Prior code uses smooth strain/seismic composites and a single dominant orientation. Smooth fields may only locate a broad region; local edges/acquisition geometry can confound. | **Low expected 100 m DTI value, high uncertainty; no forecast.** Medium cost. Core layers only. |

## External-data availability boundary

The primary H55 test needs no external file. USGS [3DEP 1 m DEM collection](https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS:77ae0551-c61e-4979-aedd-d797abdcde0e) is public domain according to the official catalog, but challenge-footprint coverage and downloadable tiles were **not** checked; it is not a viable or used H55 input until those checks pass. A [USGS ComCat count query](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/count?format=text&starttime=2019-01-01&endtime=2026-10-07&minmagnitude=2.5&minlatitude=37&maxlatitude=41&minlongitude=-120&maxlongitude=-116) returned 3,329 events for the stated bounds, but event records were not retrieved; it does not validate a sequence-corridor candidate and is not ranked as viable.

## Sources and interpretation limits

* [DrivenData problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) describes the grid/scoring context and warns known-fault labels may be incomplete or inaccurate. Catalogue-zero is not verified absence.
* [DrivenData about/data page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/) describes the supplied geophysical/surface context. Separate raster bands are not automatically independent measurements.
* [USGS integrated blind-system report](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and), [DOI 10.2172/1724080](https://doi.org/10.2172/1724080), supports integrated evidence for blind geothermal-system research, not H55 predictions or fault truth.
* [Official USGS GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and), [DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ), reports survey spacing/clearance and CC0; 100 m raster cells are not independent 100 m magnetic observations.
* [DrivenData staff clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4) says known pixels are masked pixel-exactly. It does not imply a 200 m ring earns no penalty, authenticate any owner-reported filename/score mapping, or certify a fault interpretation.
