## Frozen H55-1 validation plan

- Primary: compare a new H55-1 feature-augmented structural-contrast learner with the strongest comparable single-view baseline, View B, using the same 4 spatial quadrants, whole original 8-connected catalogue components, 80-pixel train/evaluation buffer, 36-pixel feature-support erosion, seed, catalogue-zero proxy rules, and metric-aware 37,654-pixel global budget as R2. Train/held-out components must be disjoint. No prior TIFF pixels, prior masks, public scores, external labels, or H54 revealed-preference pixels may enter features, training, model selection or placement.
- Controls: View A, View B, max/union and the pre-existing R2 structural-contrast arm at the same budget. Compare on both mean and each fold; report all paired differences and DTI components. The “best holdout” is the **measured local catalogue-recovery comparator** only, not a forecast of the hidden/new-fault leaderboard score.
- Promotion rule, frozen before the new run: the candidate must exceed the strongest comparable baseline by at least **0.005 mean DTI** and be higher in at least **3 of 4** outer folds. Otherwise: do not use a weekly submission slot. A format-valid, canonical-distinct research TIFF may still be retained and labelled “not slot-approved”; its availability is not a promotion decision.
- Co-training gate: compute A/B OOF false-positive-rate and negative-MSE correlations over finite 50×50 spatial blocks on the same held-out catalogue-zero proxies. With fewer than 20 valid blocks, undefined/constant diagnostics, or max absolute Pearson/Spearman correlation ≥0.60, disable pseudo-label exchange. If it proceeds, exchange only whole connected proposals within training-only blocks, with a 3-pixel catalogue exclusion and ≥80-pixel evaluation buffer; donor must be in its top 1% and receiver in its training-derived 40–80% abstention interval. Keep the one-round pseudo-label model separate from the primary and compare against both single views on hide/recover. Weak error correlation is a diagnostic only, **not proof** of the Blum–Mitchell conditional-independence premise.
- Output if a research artifact is built: single-band float32 on the sample grid, finite raw values in [0,1], all-finite zero background outside the sample mask, metric-aware max-cover placement, re-open-and-read format receipt, exact decoded-value comparisons against all retrievable aligned site/repo priors, same-budget A/B union comparison, and one geological-reasoning row for each emitted A-confident/B-abstaining candidate.

## Source boundary

The official competition problem description says known labels are incomplete and may be inaccurate, the hidden test contains expert-identified faults missing from the public USGS database, and the final round uses expert review of submissions. It does **not** disclose the hidden faults’ sources, geology, or spatial distribution. The USGS Death Valley geophysical summary says gravity anomalies reflect density contrasts and that aeromagnetic anomalies reflect subsurface magnetic rocks; it notes that faults offsetting magnetic lithologies can create useful anomalies while fluvial reworking can create alternatives. The USGS Amargosa aeromagnetic report describes linear anomalies associated with some shallow faults, but also points out subtle anomalies over alluvium may instead reflect alteration or fluvial concentration of magnetite. These regional studies support physical plausibility and the need for alternatives, **not transfer to every H55 pixel or a predicted DTI gain**. The local Amargosa/Death Valley reports do not cover or validate the entire competition grid. The official GEMS task and validation claims are linked in `docs/data/verified_claims_r2.json` and `docs/sources.html`.
