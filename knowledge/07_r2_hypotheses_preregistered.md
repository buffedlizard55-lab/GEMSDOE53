# R2 hypotheses — registered before new model implementation

Recorded 2026-10-06 UTC; session base `ee97b81`; see `registry/r2_preregistration.json` for fixed settings. These are hypotheses, not discoveries or numerical score forecasts. Rank reflects expected **out-of-block DTI** value per implementation cost, not a promised public score. The task is fault mapping indicative of geothermal resources, not verified geothermal vent detection.

## Audit boundary

Reviewed both `src/gems52/` and `src/gems52_h1/`. The latter already contains Hessian ridges, structure-tensor coherence, depth gradients, a conductivity edge and a buried-zone diagnostic. Calling any of those alone novel would be incorrect. Newness below refers to the specific signed/conditional features, not to rediscovering those transforms. No claim of priority outside this repository or of exhaustive novelty across inaccessible sibling artifacts is made.

## Physical support and limits (primary sources)

* [DrivenData problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/): incomplete and potentially inaccurate known faults; new expert labels; actual scoring target and 100 m grid.
* [DrivenData about page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/): faults, field/geophysical/remote-sensing methods; faults can provide fluid pathways, but this does not establish a geothermal reservoir.
* [USGS Great Basin integrated blind-system report](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and), [DOI 10.2172/1724080](https://doi.org/10.2172/1724080): integrating gravity gradients, structure, magnetic and thermal evidence can identify blind systems. This supports the mechanism, not the truth of any R2 prediction.
* [USGS GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and), [DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ): mostly 400 m flight-line spacing in Area 2, variable terrain clearance; 100 m raster cells must not be mistaken for independent 100 m magnetic measurements. Release explicitly marked CC0.
* [Hermant et al. 2025, official Stanford proceedings PDF](https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/db/GeoConf/papers/SGW/2025/Hermant.pdf): mapping-scale heterogeneity and surface scarp approaches; faults can also impede fluid flow. This is scientific literature, not an organizer disclosure about hidden labels.
* [Blum & Mitchell primary paper](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~avrim/Papers/cotrain.pdf), [DOI 10.1145/279943.279962](https://doi.org/10.1145/279943.279962): the sufficient-view and conditional-independence assumptions are not guaranteed for a buried fault with no surface expression. A weak proxy error correlation is NOT proof of conditional feature independence.

## Decision before implementation

Test R2-H1 first. Do not request or consume a DrivenData upload slot. Whole connected fault components must not be split between train and evaluation; use spatial held-out regions and a buffer of at least twice the maximum transform support. Negatives are **catalogue-zero proxies**, not field-verified absence. Obtain OOF predictions on those negatives, unlike the previous empty-negative report. Strong correlation (absolute Pearson or tie-aware Spearman >= 0.60), too few usable blocks, or constant errors disables pseudo-label exchange. No fallback to positive-only correlation.

A predeclared supervised structural-contrast model is a legitimate fallback if co-training is disabled, but must be named as such. Compare with separate views, raw early fusion and their max/union at identical emission density. The exported file must be evaluated with the same metric-aware placement used in validation, re-opened for format checks, checked for pixel novelty (not just a filename/hash change), and have a reasoning row for every A-only candidate. An experimental artifact may be downloadable even after a failed model gate, but must not be described as approved for a weekly slot. No forced promotion.
