**Status at registration:** four geological hypotheses reviewed; H58-A is the only candidate runnable from the currently staged inputs. This document and `registry/h58_preregistration.json` must be committed before an H58 runner or H58 holdout code is added. ## 1. Verified task and data boundary

The official DrivenData problem page describes a fault-mapping task, says the known fault data may be incomplete or inaccurate, and says the initial private truth consists of expert-labelled faults not in the public USGS map. It defines a 300 m triangular distance kernel, `alpha=0.2`, `beta=0.8`, and requires one float32 GeoTIFF on the EPSG:32611, 100 m grid with values in `[0,1]`. That is the task and metric; this repo's catalogue holdout is only a proxy and cannot validate private-truth performance or a leaderboard score. [Official problem/metric/format](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/).

The official INGENIOUS GDR submission 1391 lists well/spring temperatures and aqueous chemistry, paleogeothermal sinter/tufa features, and a CC BY 4.0 license. It links the 19.85 MB well/spring geodatabase and the 82.04 kB paleo-feature archive. The page verifies the **resource listing and licence statement**, not that the owner-derived CSV fields below were calculated correctly. The raw GDB was not downloaded in this sandbox. [GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391).

### Input-integrity incident — do not overlook

The 23-file owner-mirror restore is complete and byte/hash verified in `work/h58_pinned/restore_receipt.json`. However, the three tracked rasters under `data/` do **not** match those pins. A read-only decoded comparison, recorded in `evidence/h58_preflight_integrity.json`, found:

- `training_features.tif`: same grid profile, but 97,207,447 of 233,304,040 decoded values differ; 97,120,016 differences are where both copies have valid values, and 87,431 cells differ in validity. The tracked 3.36 MB file and staged 418.9 MB file are not compression variants of one another.
- `labels.tif`: same grid profile and same class counts, but 120,650 pixels differ. The two positive masks intersect at only 663 pixels; 60,325 positives occur in each copy but not the other.
- `sample_submission.tif`: same grid geometry and finite mask, but the tracked all-zero sample and staged sample disagree at 60,988 cells. All 60,988 staged ones coincide with staged labels. That conflicts with the official problem page's description of the sample as predicting total fault absence.

No tracked TIFF was changed. The staged mirror is integrity-verified **against the repo's manifest only**, not organizer-authenticated. H57's report does not bind its fitted arrays to input SHA-256 values, so its old numerical results are not a reproducible baseline for these staged bytes; they are withdrawn from H58's comparison set pending a fresh pinned-data rerun. H58 must use the exact staging directory and record the hashes it reads. Use the sample raster only for CRS/shape/transform and its finite grid domain; never copy its prediction values. This mismatch is a hard provenance limitation: even a local holdout win cannot authorize a competition upload until the input provenance and label alignment are resolved.

### Chemistry-proposal audit and novelty boundary

The thermal/geochemical area is not new in general. `knowledge/02_hypotheses_H52.md` H52-5 proposed spring-line density but left it blocked on unverified provenance. H55 implemented a GDR-derived thermal layer: `src/gems55/thermal.py` takes the **maximum** of quartz, chalcedony, and cation estimates, falls back to measured discharge temperature, retains values at or above 60°C, recomputes distance from the labels, and extends points along a structure-tensor strike. H55's `knowledge/03_negative_results_and_what_they_killed.md` N-20 records the injected thermal layer as neutral on the registered 37,654-pixel selection (difference +0.00003). H56-THERMAL-SPRING remained a proposal for hot-point proximity/strike corroboration, not a validated result. H54's radiometric alteration-ratio interpretation was tested and mostly refuted as a standalone detector. No implemented code or earlier proposal found in this checkout uses a **cold observed discharge plus agreement of two distinct reservoir-geothermometer estimates** as the point-quality rule.

The earlier uncommitted H58 junction/termination draft is withdrawn: R3-H2 and H55-JUNCTION/H56-JUNCTION already cover that topological family. The slate below replaces it before H58 implementation; no claim of priority beyond this repository is made. The exact H58-A statistic remains a hypothesis, not a discovery.

The staged well/spring CSV has 27,092 rows and 12 columns. Its quartz and cation estimates are owner-mirror-derived values; it has no raw major-ion chemistry. For the frozen H58-A rule below, a pre-holdout coverage check found 22 qualifying rows at 20 unique grid cells. `dist_known_fault_px` is a label-derived column and is **never read or used** by H58.

## 3. Frozen H58-A protocol (registered before implementation/results)

### Inputs and reproducibility

1. Use `work/h58_pinned` as the data root. Before any feature build or scoring, verify the manifest file itself against the SHA-256 frozen in `registry/h58_preregistration.json`, then verify every required input against that manifest; the runner must fail closed on a missing file or SHA/byte mismatch. Record input IDs, byte counts and SHA-256 values in the result receipt. Never overwrite the tracked `data/*.tif` files. State that a matching owner-mirror pin is not organizer authentication.
2. Build the separate views from the checked-in H57 layer specification: View A potential-field/subsurface channels; View B DEM and available radiometric/LiDAR channels. Use separate balanced logistic learners and the same seed/sample indices per fold. Both positive and negative training samples must lie inside the fold's `fit` mask; negative clearance is computed only from catalogue labels visible inside that fit mask, never from held-out labels. Use exact staged labels. The pinned sample raster supplies only grid metadata and the finite domain; its cell values are not predictions and are never used as a feature.
3. Read only `row`, `col`, `temp_c`, `geothermquartz_c`, and `geothermcat_c` from the well/spring CSV. Do not load or reference `dist_known_fault_px`. At each unique row/column site, qualify rows with `temp_c <= 30`, both geothermometers `>= 100`, and absolute difference `<= 20`. Keep each qualifying site once. No missing-value fallback, hot-sample fallback, temperature threshold sweep, or post-result tuning.
4. Define `F_geo(x) = max_s max(1 − d(x,s)/5,000 m, 0)` over qualified sites, on the all-band-valid training footprint. This is the **sole H58-A rank field**. It is a sparse point-evidence field, not a fault-probability calibration. No distance to known faults, prior prediction values, label-trained field, strike extension, or post-hoc weight enters it.

### Views, errors, and pseudo-label rule

5. Generate OOF View-A and View-B probabilities using four fixed contiguous quadrants. Keep every original 8-connected catalogue component whole and assign it to the quadrant containing most of its pixels. In a block fold, if a component assigned elsewhere crosses into the scored quadrant, quarantine that entire component from this fold's fit and visible-label mask; it is not added to this fold's scored truth. For block error diagnostics, train outside the held quadrant, exclude an 80-pixel collar at fold/footprint boundaries, and predict the held quadrant. Use 50×50-pixel error blocks with at least 300 catalogue-zero proxy negatives. Report block MSE and false-positive-rate correlations, Pearson and tie-aware Spearman. If fewer than 20 usable non-degenerate blocks remain, any required statistic is undefined, or any absolute correlation is `>= 0.60`, fail closed: **abandon co-training and do not pseudo-label**. Weak correlation is not proof of conditional independence.
6. If and only if the registered independence gate passes, run at most one diagnostic exchange per outer quadrant. The evaluation quadrant is never used to train or select pseudo-labels. For each outer fold, reserve the next quadrant cyclically as a disjoint, unlabeled pseudo-region; fit the two donor views on the other two quadrants only, excluding an 80-pixel boundary collar and visible-label buffers. Accept only whole 8-connected components entirely inside one 50×50 block, at least 5 pixels, no known-label/corridor pixels, and at least 80 pixels from evaluation. Donor must be `>=0.60`; receiver must lie in `[0.40,0.60]` (abstention, not confident negative). Cap at 2,000 pixels per receiver/view/fold; use pseudo weight 0.25. Evaluate pre/post AUC on all primary-held components versus catalogue-zero proxy negatives at least 500 m from every catalogue pixel, both within the edge-cleared scored region, and evaluate same-fold, same-budget DTI. Do not let the training boundary collar remove the evaluation pixels themselves. The exchange is diagnostic only and is not used to alter H58-A's fixed geochemical rank field.
7. Separately from H58-A, make one A-only diagnostic emission from the block-OOF maps: rank `p_A` only inside `p_A >= 0.60 and p_B <= 0.40`, using the same legal pool, isotropic 3-pixel separation, and primary 37,654-pixel budget (record any support shortfall). This diagnostic does not alter H58-A's geochemical field. Write one reasoning CSV row for every emitted A-only diagnostic pixel, including coordinates, measured probabilities, band-15 cover and band-19 surface context, nearest qualified-site distance, a specific buried-structure rationale, and an explicit alternative/falsifier. Never call these verified faults. Do not write the label-derived distance field to this report.

### Holdout, comparable controls, and slot decision

8. Refit views within each holdout fold; do not score an in-sample probability. Use four whole-component folds and both `hide` (entire components removed from labels, score over the footprint) and `block` (quadrant held out, train only outside it) modes, each with an 80-pixel buffer. Use prevalence `0.002` of each fold's scored region (full valid footprint for `hide`; held quadrant plus 3-pixel kernel halo for `block`), seeded whole-component thinning, seed `20261007`, visible-label handling from the registered H58 fold builder, and the same 300 m official DTI kernel. Catalogue-zero is a proxy for absence. For each mode/fold, compare `F_geo`, View A, View B, `max(p_A,p_B)` union, the A-only diagnostic, and a seeded random field. All arms share the same legal emission pool, isotropic metric-aware 3-pixel minimum-separation emitter, and budgets of 37,654 (primary) and 15,000 (secondary); scale the block-mode budget by the held region's share of the eligible footprint. H58-A may emit only inside its frozen `F_geo > 0` support and may not fill a support shortfall with zero-score pixels; a shortfall fails budget comparability. Report all fold rows and paired differences.
9. The comparable baseline is the strongest of View A, View B, or their max/union, re-fitted on these **same staged inputs and folds**. H58-A opens even a *research promotion* gate only if at 37,654 global-equivalent pixels its mean DTI exceeds that strongest baseline by at least `+0.005`, wins at least 3/4 folds against it in **both** hide and block, and all integrity/format/uniqueness/reasoning checks pass. Otherwise the hypothesis is a measured failure; do not substitute another candidate or tune its thresholds after seeing results. Passing local catalogue holdout does not authorize an upload because the holdout is not organizer-authenticated and H58-A's derived geochemistry provenance remains unresolved.
10. Generate a single-band float32 `[0,1]` TIFF from the frozen field only after the holdout, on the staged sample's EPSG:32611 grid, exact shape/transform, finite support only. Emit 37,654 nodes at 3-pixel metric-aware separation where support permits; record any shortfall. Read the written bytes back. Compare decoded pixels to every accessible aligned prior; separately compare against the same-budget View-A/View-B emissions and their union. A different hash/compression is not uniqueness. If any gate fails, the TIFF may be downloadable for research but must say **NOT approved to submit** and must not change a weekly slot.
