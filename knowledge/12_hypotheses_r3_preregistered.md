# R3 candidate hypotheses — frozen before implementation

**Registered:** 2026-10-07 UTC, before adding or evaluating the R3 transform. **Base checkout:** `a381eb2f57ffbfc90dafce5692f05c571ddc211b`. **Scope:** compare candidates against the current best *comparable local* spatial holdout arm, not against the public leaderboard. The public board exposes team-level scores only; it does not authenticate any filename/hash mapping.

The competition target is faults that may be indicative of geothermal resources; it is **not** a labelled map of geothermal vents or commercially viable reservoirs. The official problem page says the known fault map is incomplete and may contain inaccuracies, and that Phase 1 test faults were separately mapped by experts. The local catalogue-holdout is therefore only a proxy test, not hidden-test validation.

## Why R3-H1 was selected, and the preregistered test

R3-H1 uses only the restored 19-band competition raster, so it can be evaluated without guessing at external coverage or licence terms. The feature will be defined before fitting as follows: Gaussian-normalized smoothing of band 12 at sigma 2 px; local DEM-gradient unit normal; bilinear samples at ±2 px (200 m) from each pixel; left/right profile slopes; two derived features, signed pair-concordance and signed left/right shoulder asymmetry. Support is at most 10 px, less than the existing 36 px feature-support erosion and the 80 px train/evaluation buffer.

Use the existing deterministic 4-quadrant, whole original 8-connected component folds; include held component tails in the evaluation region; do not share a positive component across train and evaluation; keep the 80 px training buffer. Negatives remain *catalogue-zero proxies*, not field-verified absence. Fit the same fixed HGB learner on the same sampled fold rows for: (i) `view_B` alone (incumbent local control), and (ii) `view_B` plus only the two registered R3-H1 features. Use the existing metric implementation and equal positive-pixel budget scaled by eligible fold area. Report every fold, paired lift, feature support, data hashes and test receipt. No post-hoc tuning or changed budget is allowed in this test.

Also report the two-view error-correlation diagnostic on actual held-out catalogue-zero proxy negatives, with block-level continuous Brier/MSE and false-positive-rate errors, Pearson and tie-aware Spearman. If any registered correlation is undefined, there are fewer than 20 usable spatial blocks, or any absolute correlation is at least 0.60, **do not exchange pseudo-labels**. If that gate passes, a one-round whole-segment confident-donor/abstaining-receiver experiment is reported separately and cannot replace the registered primary. Previous R2 co-training did not promote; weak proxy error correlation is not proof of conditional independence.

**Submission-slot rule:** a local result must clear the +0.005 / 3-of-4-fold comparison to the strongest same-protocol control before it is even considered. That alone does not prove a public-score gain: the local catalogue-holdout instrument is known not to predict the organiser's ranking reliably, and this repo does not possess the hidden evaluation labels. No portal slot is used by this experiment.

## Official sources checked for this ranking

- [DrivenData problem description, metric and submission format](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) — faults are the target; known labels are incomplete/inaccurate; the metric is distance-weighted; the raster requires EPSG:32611, 100 m, matching bounds, one float32 band with values in [0,1].
- [DrivenData about page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/) — faults are discontinuities with displacement; topography, geophysics and remote sensing are evidence sources, not standalone fault verification.
- [USGS GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and), DOI [10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ) — survey products and resolution/flight-height context. The release says Area 2 flight lines are 400 m apart and terrain clearance varies; 100 m pixels are not independent 100 m magnetic measurements.
- [USGS blind geothermal systems report](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and), DOI [10.2172/1724080](https://doi.org/10.2172/1724080) — examples of transfer zones and terminating/intersecting gravity gradients within integrated site studies; not evidence about the competition's hidden labels.
- [USGS 1 m 3DEP DEM catalog](https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS:77ae0551-c61e-4979-aedd-d797abdcde0e) and [The National Map Downloader](https://apps.nationalmap.gov/downloader/) — official public-domain source metadata and an acquisition route; exact footprint coverage/bytes remain unverified here.
- [INGENIOUS GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391), DOI [10.15121/1881483](https://doi.org/10.15121/1881483) — public CC BY 4.0 regional resources including shallow-temperature probes, paleo-geothermal features and wells/springs. This is contextual evidence for geothermal systems, not a direct fault label; no new GDR resource bytes were used in R3-H1.
- [Blum & Mitchell, COLT 1998](https://doi.org/10.1145/279943.279962), author-hosted [paper PDF](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~avrim/Papers/cotrain.pdf) — co-training is conditional on compatible/sufficient views and its theoretical assumptions; their validity here is not assumed.

## Limitations and falsification conditions

The data mirror is integrity-pinned but not authenticated against an organizer download. Spatial folds reveal recovery of visible catalogue components, not discovery of the private new-fault test set. A low correlation between view errors on catalogue-zero pixels cannot prove conditional independence because hidden faults may be mislabeled negatives and the views share the same grid/geology. A one-meter source is patchy and the exact link list is not locally available. A fault is not automatically permeable, hot or a geothermal vent. Any A-only row is a model hypothesis with lithologic, depth-model, acquisition and surface-process alternatives; no model output is geological verification.
