## Why H33 (0.2778) was the best among the listed GEMSDOE sites

**Source:** `docs/forensics.html` and `evidence/reference_forensics_r2.json` (hash-verified bytes).

- The file `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif` (H33) is derived from `h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8` (base, 40,199 px) by **deleting 2,545 off-catalogue flank dots within 200 m of known traces**, adding 0. None were on the known-fault mask (verified 0), all were off-mask within 200 m. The edit is exactly `base AND distance_to_catalogue >200m`.
- Staff confirm known pixels are masked and **do not pay a penalty** (so pruning on-mask cannot help). Off-mask flank mass can still be FP if its incremental max-cover `c` is small relative to its FP mass `f`. For a 300 m triangular kernel `k(d)=max(1-d/300,0)`, an addition helps iff `c*(1-0.2*DTI) > 0.2*DTI*f`. At DTI=0.2778, isolated bar is 0.05556; the 283 m diagonal weight 0.05719 clears it, but flank dots near a known trace have high `max_g k(d)` already covered, so `c` is tiny and they are net negative. Pruning them reduces FPw without losing much TPw.
- H33’s placement efficiency `A/S` is 8.044 (85.8% of the exact 9.380298 kernel-disc ceiling) vs H56’s synthetic 8+ and H55’s 8.815 (94%). Its gain is **not** from adding new geology but from **better FP/FN trade-off via near-catalogue pruning**.
- **How to beat it:** New placement must be supported by independent physics, not another blanket union or wider buffer. Whole-component spatial holdout with a buffered buffer (80 px) is required; random splits leak. The remaining ~0.04 gap to the board top (0.3195 was rank 7; 0.3774 was observed leader 2026-10-06) is likely **geological domain knowledge, not post-processing** — e.g., radiometric lithology, LiDAR scarps, or spring chemistry that the broad potential-field + narrow DEM baseline misses.

**Official DTI:** `TPw / (TPw + 0.2*FPw + 0.8*FNw)` with `k(d)=max(1-d/300,0)`, staff-verified that known faults are masked and that public/private scores pool all subset pixels into one Tversky index. See `docs/forensics.html` for the corrected incremental analysis (the old “224 m” universal rule was wrong).

## Preregistered test for H56-COTRAIN-BURIED (synthetic demo)

1. **Spatial blocks + buffer:** whole 8-connected catalogue components, 80 px (≈8 km? Actually 80 px = 8 km? No, 80 px = 8000 m) buffer? Wait, prompt says `buffer so no leakage reaches evaluation`. Our synthetic uses 50×50 blocks and 3 px (300 m) kernel awareness, but real test must use **whole-component quadrant folds** (NE/NW/SE/SW) with **80 px buffer** as in `src/gems52/spatial.py` (`folds` with `buffer_px=80`). Keep the same eligible footprint (all-19-band intersection), proxy-negative definition (40 per positive, 300 m collar), random seed, logistic learner (L2, `C=1.0`, `class_weight=balanced`), prior adjustment, metric kernel (`k(d)=max(1-d/300,0)`), global-equivalent emitted density, and `greedy_emit` with `dti=0` (fixed matched budget). Hold out **complete 8-connected catalogue components**; proxy negatives are “not in catalogue”, not geological absence.

2. **Compare at identical per-fold budgets:** View B baseline (DEM + radiometric TC), View A baseline (potential-field), and H56-COTRAIN (disagreement-weighted density). Report every fold, DTI, paired differences, plus spatial ROC as secondary. Candidate-vs-baseline must be based on **fold DTI, not AUC**.

3. **Promotion criteria (frozen):** candidate mean lift **≥0.005** over strongest comparable baseline, improvement in **≥3/4 folds**, leakage checks pass (`shared_train_truth_components==0`, `train ∩ region == ∅`), and output format/uniqueness/not-union gates pass. Because the repository documents that its historical holdout has **weak/negative correspondence with the public board** (Spearman −0.1045, p=0.734, n=13), passing this proxy gate still does **not** establish an organizer score or justify a claim of beating 0.3195. **No weekly slot is spent on synthetic.**

4. **If it fails, retain research-only TIFF only if useful for reproducibility, mark “NOT approved for upload”, record the negative result and do not select a weaker alternative post hoc.** If feature support, folds, or score calculations are invalid, stop without writing a TIFF.

## Result / status — run completed 2026-10-07T16:39Z (synthetic demo)

H56-COTRAIN was implemented as a **synthetic demonstration** because `data/training_features.tif` is not present in this sandbox (requires `bash scripts/download_competition_data.sh` on an unrestricted machine). The synthetic View A/B fields were generated with **broad vs narrow smoothing** and **independent fault-like lines** (8 for A, 12 for B, plus 5 radiometric patches), yielding **weak correlation 0.0019** on valid pixels.

- **Independence test (synthetic, 50×50 blocks, 4672 blocks ≥32 negatives):** `max |Spearman| = 0.0075` vs `ABANDON_R=0.60` → **proceed** (`allow_exchange=True`). This is a proxy diagnostic, not proof of conditional independence (IR-52-019).
- **Strata:** 65,599 A-only, 64,214 B-only, 1,101 concordant, 12,148,246 silent (42.07% footprint).
- **Density:** A-only weighted 0.7×(0.5+0.5×cover), concordant 0.25, B-only 0.05×0.3; plus 1% prob_a base; mean 0.014.
- **Emission:** `greedy_emit` with `dti=0`, budget 37,654, pool 400k → **37,654 emitted**, all 8-isolated, total expected credit 40,205.
- **Gates (synthetic):** format **PASS** (float32, [0,1], 3730×3292, EPSG:32611, 0 NaN), uniqueness **PASS** (96.7% novel vs 20 priors, 36417 novel, 327930 prior px dropped), not-union **PASS** (37.1% vs topK union, 6.4% vs greedy union).
- **Reasoning:** 381 300-m neighborhoods, **372 A-only groups** covering 37,390 A-only px, each with East/North, cover depth, probs, and a **buried-fault vs lithologic-contact alternative**.
- **Holdout (synthetic, not real):** View B 0.03948 vs cotrain 0.09701 (from H55 hide) — **illustrative only**, not a real spatial holdout. **No weekly slot used.**
