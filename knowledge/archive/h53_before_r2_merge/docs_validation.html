Validation: three instruments, and what each can and cannot see

Every claim here is a number from evidence/, produced on folds built from the official label file, with the visible catalogue masked out of both the allowed emission set and the score — the same treatment the organiser applies (thread 11516, posts #2 and #4, chrisk-dd).

1 Why one instrument is not enough

instrumentwhat is hiddenheld truth within 5 px of a still-visible tracewhat it can measurewhat it is blind to

The third column is measured on the real label file, not assumed (knowledge/05_instruments_and_what_each_can_see.md). It is why this repo runs two instruments and selects the configuration that is not the worst on either (scripts/budget_sweep.py) rather than the one that maximises the flattering number.

The promotion rule, applied literally. registry/preregistration.json requires the shipped arm to beat the naive union by 0.010 DTI on the folds and beat uniform-random emission at the same budget on ≥ 3 of 4 folds. The co-training arm failed on both instruments — beats_union: not OK, placement_not_mass: not OK, fold_support: 0/4 — so it is not what we ship, and no weekly slot was spent on it.
